# Privacy Policy — Livebars

**Last updated:** May 13, 2026

## Summary

Livebars does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device and in your private iCloud account.

## What Livebars stores

Livebars stores the data you enter in the app — your needs, aspirations, treatments, daily tasks, and activity history. This data is saved:

- **Locally on your device** (in the app's own sandboxed storage).
- **In your private iCloud account** (via Apple's CloudKit framework), so it stays in sync between your iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

Your iCloud data is encrypted end to end by Apple. The developer of Livebars has no access to it.

## What Livebars does NOT do

- No analytics, telemetry, or usage tracking.
- No advertising or ad networks.
- No third-party SDKs.
- No accounts, no servers, no login.
- No data is ever sent to the developer or to any third party.

## Permissions

Livebars may ask for the following permissions, all of which are optional:

- **Notifications** — only to remind you about treatments and tasks you've scheduled. No marketing or engagement notifications.

## Children

Livebars is rated 13+ due to references to wellness, supplements, and adult lifestyle topics. It is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated date at the top will reflect it. Substantive changes will also be communicated within the app.
